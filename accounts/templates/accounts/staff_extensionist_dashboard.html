📚 Dashboard Guide

🎯 Getting Started

Wave Emoji (👋): Click the wave emoji next to your name to show/hide the upload and edit controls.

Info Icons (ℹ️): Hover over any info icon to get quick tips about that feature.

📤 Uploading MOA Files

1. Click the wave emoji to reveal controls

2. Click "Upload New Partner" button

3. Fill in the title, select logo image and PDF file

4. Click "Upload" to save

✏️ Editing & Deleting MOAs

1. Click the wave emoji to reveal controls

2. Click "Show / Hide Delete" button

3. Delete links will appear under each MOA logo

📊 Understanding Document Status

Pending: Awaiting review

Recommended: Approved by coordinator, awaiting staff review

Approved: Fully approved, ready for implementation

Ongoing: Currently in progress

Completed: Finished and archived

🔄 Revision Tracking

Click on any department box in the "Department Revisions" section to see which files need revision.

Click the document name to view details and re-upload corrected files.

💡 Pro Tips

• Check the "All Document Updates" section daily for recent changes

• Use color-coded status boxes to quickly identify document states

• Hover over any ℹ️ icon for context-specific help

• Press '?' on your keyboard to open this help modal anytime

• Press 'Escape' to close any modal
